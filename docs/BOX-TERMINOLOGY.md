# Buyer-facing box terminology

House of Tartufo Price List uses **box** as the commercial packaging term in buyer-facing UI.

- English: `Box` / `Boxes`
- Italian: `box`
- French: `box`
- Dutch: `box` / `boxen`
- `Case pack` is presented as units per box.
- Internal data-contract identifiers such as `unitsPerCase`, `minCases`, and pricing variables remain unchanged to preserve compatibility with the official master and pricing engine.

This rule applies to catalogue headers, volume pricing, quantity controls, quote lines, accessibility labels, and product-detail specifications.
